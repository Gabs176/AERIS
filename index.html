02 / Why AERIS

Engineering is the starting point.
Value is the goal.

We believe automotive electronics should be built with a clear path from functional hardware to useful, scalable solutions.

AERIS begins with hands-on PCB and embedded-systems development. The next step is to validate which connected-vehicle problems are most worth solving — with customers, measurable requirements, and disciplined iteration.
